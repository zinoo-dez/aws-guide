<section id="business-problem">
  <h2>1. Business Problem & Context</h2>
  <p>
    A multinational retail enterprise maintains an internal analytics portal used exclusively by employees between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM on weekdays. The previous architecture ran 12 high-memory instances (<code>r6i.2xlarge</code>) 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, wasting over 65% of its compute budget overnight and on weekends.
  </p>
</section>

<section id="requirements-constraints">
  <h2>2. Requirements & Constraints</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Predictable Business Hour Readiness</strong>: Capacity must be fully warmed up by 7:45 AM Monday–Friday.</li>
    <li><strong>Significant Cost Reduction</strong>: Target > 60% compute budget savings.</li>
    <li><strong>Interruption Resilience</strong>: Seamlessly handle Spot Instance termination notices using mixed instance fleets.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section id="implementation">
  <h2>6. Implementation Highlights</h2>
  <DeepDiveAccordion title="Scheduled Scaling Cron Expressions" badge="Configuration">

```
# Morning Scale-Up (Monday through Friday at 7:45 AM UTC)
Recurrence: "45 7 * * 1-5"
DesiredCapacity: 12, MinSize: 4, MaxSize: 20

# Evening Scale-Down (Monday through Friday at 6:30 PM UTC)
Recurrence: "30 18 * * 1-5"
DesiredCapacity: 0, MinSize: 0, MaxSize: 2
```
