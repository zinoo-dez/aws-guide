<section id="business-problem">
  <h2>1. Business Problem & Context</h2>
  <p>
    A global video streaming service streams content to over 15 million subscribers across Smart TVs, mobile devices, and web browsers. When a blockbuster season premiered, 2.5 million users clicked "Play" simultaneously. The previous monolithic transcoding and streaming server architecture collapsed under bandwidth saturation, and mobile users on slow cellular connections suffered severe video buffering.
  </p>
</section>

<section id="requirements-constraints">
  <h2>2. Requirements & Constraints</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Adaptive Bitrate Streaming (ABR)</strong>: Transcode raw master video (ProRes / 4K MP4) into 7 distinct HLS bitrates (from 1080p 6Mbps down to 360p 400Kbps).</li>
    <li><strong>Sub-400ms Video Startup Time</strong>: First video segment (.m4s) must reach player in &lt;400ms globally.</li>
    <li><strong>DRM & Tokenized Access</strong>: Prevent unauthorized video hotlinking and screen scraping using CloudFront Signed Cookies.</li>
    <li><strong>Multi-Region Resume Sync</strong>: Allow users to pause on a Smart TV in New York and resume on a phone in London with exact second accuracy.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section id="architecture-overview">
  <h2>3. Architecture Overview & Data Flow</h2>
  
  <ArchitectureDiagram 
    title="Global End-to-End Media Ingestion & Delivery Architecture"
    diagram={`graph TD

    S3Mezz --> StepFn[AWS Step Functions Media Orchestrator]

        StepFn --> MediaConvert[AWS Elemental MediaConvert: HLS/DASH]

    end

    MediaConvert -->|Output HLS Packages| S3Delivery[(S3 Delivery Origin Bucket)]
    
    subgraph EdgeDeliveryTier [Global Edge Network]
        S3Delivery --> OriginShield[CloudFront Origin Shield: us-east-1]
        OriginShield --> RegionalEdge[Regional Edge Caches: Europe, Asia, US]
        RegionalEdge --> ViewerPoP[450+ Edge PoPs]
    end

    Viewer([Subscriber Smart TV / Mobile]) -->|Signed Cookie Auth| ViewerPoP
    Viewer -->|Sync Playback Progress| DDBGlobal[(DynamoDB Global Tables)]

  <ol>
    <li><strong>Master Upload</strong>: Studio uploads 4K ProRes master file to S3 Mezzanine bucket via accelerated S3 Transfer Acceleration.</li>
    <li><strong>Orchestrated Transcoding</strong>: AWS Step Functions triggers AWS Elemental MediaConvert with accelerated transcoding, generating 7 adaptive bitrate streams and fragmented MP4 packages.</li>
    <li><strong>Origin Shield Consolidation</strong>: CloudFront Origin Shield caches video segments, reducing origin read load by 99.85% even during viral premieres.</li>
    <li><strong>Global Playback State</strong>: Playback positions (e.g. <code>timestamp: 01:24:18</code>) sync to Amazon DynamoDB Global Tables with sub-second replication across `us-east-1`, `eu-west-1`, and `ap-northeast-1`.</li>
  </ol>
