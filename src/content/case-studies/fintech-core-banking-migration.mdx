<section id="business-problem">
  <h2>1. Business Problem & Context</h2>
  <p>
    A digital commercial bank processes over $40 Billion in daily transactions. Central bank regulatory mandates required a maximum <strong>Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of 0 seconds</strong> (zero financial transaction loss) and a <strong>Recovery Time Objective (RTO) under 60 seconds</strong> in the catastrophic event that an entire AWS geographical region (e.g. all AZs in <code>us-east-1</code>) experiences complete loss of power or connectivity.
  </p>
</section>

<section id="requirements-constraints">
  <h2>2. Requirements & Constraints</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Active-Active Multi-Region</strong>: Users in both East and West coasts write to their nearest regional endpoint simultaneously.</li>
    <li><strong>Deterministic Conflict Resolution</strong>: Last-writer-wins conflict resolution with logical version numbers on bank accounts.</li>
    <li><strong>Zero-DNS Propagation Failover</strong>: Route 53 ARC routing controls must reroute 100% of traffic within 10 seconds without waiting for TTL expiration.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
