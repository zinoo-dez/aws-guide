<section id="business-problem">
  <h2>1. Business Problem & Context</h2>
  <p>
    An online banking platform experienced customer-facing downtime whenever a new release had a hidden runtime bug. Standard in-place rolling deployments updated all instances simultaneously, exposing 100% of live users to 500 Internal Server Errors before engineers could manually intervene.
  </p>
</section>

<section id="requirements-constraints">
  <h2>2. Requirements & Constraints</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Zero In-Flight Connection Drops</strong>: Use ALB connection draining (deregistration delay).</li>
    <li><strong>Progressive Canary Shifting</strong>: Route 10% of real user traffic for 10 minutes to test stability.</li>
    <li><strong>Automated Instant Rollback</strong>: If HTTP 5XX errors exceed 1%, abort and revert 100% of traffic to Blue within 30 seconds.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section id="takeaways">
  <h2>8. Key Architectural Takeaways</h2>
  <blockquote>
    <strong>Deployment Rule:</strong> Never deploy 100% of new code directly to all production servers. Always route a 10% canary slice while monitoring CloudWatch 5XX alarms for automated rollback.
  </blockquote>
